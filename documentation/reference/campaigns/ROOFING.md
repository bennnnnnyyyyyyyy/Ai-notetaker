# Campaign: Prime Ad Roofing Appointment Setting

## Identity

- ID: `roofing_appointment_setting`
- Version: `1.0`
- Active: yes
- Description: Book discovery meetings with roofing contractors that want outsourced outbound calling for homeowner inspection and estimate appointments.

## Ideal Customer Profile

- Industries: Residential and light-commercial roofing, including replacement, repair, and storm restoration.
- Company types: Licensed and insured contractors with sales or estimating staff.
- Company size: 5–100 employees; owner or sales manager is the decision maker.
- Geography: United States; priority TX, FL, CA.
- Exclusions: Unlicensed/uninsured contractors, no capacity for estimates, non-local storm chasers, or no sales staff.

## Qualification Criteria

### Business fit — required

Evidence: license, insurance, service area, and roofing types. Pass when licensed/insured and operating in a target or agreed expansion area.

### Relevant need — required

Evidence: inspection/estimate appointment gap or slow lead follow-up. Pass when a target volume is missed or a lead list/ad spend needs follow-up.

### Capacity to convert — required

Evidence: estimator/sales-rep count, average close rate, and average job value. Pass when at least one appointment owner and average job value are confirmed.

### Authority and timing — required

Evidence: role and buying process. Pass when the speaker decides or commits to include the owner/decision maker.

## Meeting Objectives

1. Confirm service area, job types, average ticket, close rate, and lead sources.
2. Understand appointment setting and where leads are lost.
3. Present hourly and pay-per-appointment options against stated numbers.
4. Secure a pilot, proposal, or dated owner follow-up.

## Opener Scorecard

- Booking quality: owner/decision maker, date/time, and license status confirmed.
- Information accuracy: approved pricing/service claims only; no guarantees.
- Expectation setting: caller, purpose, and duration are clear.
- Pre-meeting capture: service area, estimator count, sources, and decision maker.

## Closer Scorecard

- Discovery: average ticket, close rate, sources, and follow-up speed.
- Presentation: pricing tied to the prospect’s numbers.
- Objection handling: price, quality, and script-control concerns addressed.
- Qualification: license, need, capacity, and authority evidenced.
- Closing: pilot, proposal, or dated follow-up with named owner.

## Report Rules

- Support facts with transcript evidence and mark unknowns explicitly.
- Interest is not commitment.
- Quote job value and close-rate figures exactly as stated.
- Pricing must match the approved offer; otherwise mark it not quoted.
