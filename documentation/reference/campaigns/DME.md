# Campaign: Prime Ad DME Lead Generation

## Identity

- ID: `dme_lead_generation`
- Version: `1.0`
- Active: yes
- Description: Book discovery meetings with DME suppliers that need qualified, doctor-referred patients and outsourced acquisition/intake calling.

## Ideal Customer Profile

- Industries: Durable medical equipment, including braces/orthotics, CGM, mobility, respiratory, and diabetic supplies.
- Company types: Independent DME suppliers and small multi-location DME groups with billing and Medicare/insurance enrollment.
- Company size: 3–100 employees; owner, president, or sales/marketing director is the decision maker.
- Geography: United States; priority FL, TX, CA, GA.
- Exclusions: No active NPI/accreditation, OIG LEIE exclusion, hospital-owned department without vendor authority, or active payer/CMS investigation.

## Qualification Criteria

### Business fit — required

Evidence: prospect confirms DME status, product lines, and active billing entity. Pass when at least one currently billed DME line and NPI/accreditation are confirmed.

### Relevant need — required

Evidence: lead/referral/patient-volume gap or dissatisfaction with a paid source. Pass when more qualified patients/leads are needed or leads are actively purchased.

### Authority and timing — required

Evidence: role and buying process. Pass when the speaker decides or agrees to include the decision maker.

### Compliance readiness — optional

Evidence: physician-order and documentation process. Pass when the prospect confirms they can process leads compliantly.

## Meeting Objectives

1. Confirm product lines, monthly lead/order volume, lead sources, and cost per lead.
2. Identify pain points such as lead quality, intake speed, denials, or referral drop-off.
3. Connect Prime Ad’s process to the prospect’s stated needs.
4. Secure a pilot, proposal, or dated follow-up with the decision maker.

## Opener Scorecard

- Booking quality: decision maker, confirmed date/time, product lines confirmed.
- Information accuracy: approved messaging only; no guarantees or invented numbers.
- Expectation setting: who is meeting, purpose, and duration are clear.
- Pre-meeting capture: product lines, volume, vendors, and decision maker recorded.

## Closer Scorecard

- Discovery: volume, sources, cost per lead, conversion, denial/fallout rates.
- Presentation: tied to stated pain, not a generic pitch.
- Objection handling: price, quality, and compliance addressed specifically.
- Qualification: fit, need, authority, and compliance supported by evidence.
- Closing: next step has date, owner, and deliverable.

## Report Rules

- Support facts with transcript evidence.
- Mark unknown information unknown.
- Interest is not commitment.
- Quote volume and cost figures exactly as stated; do not estimate.
