# Campaign: Prime Ad CGM Patient Acquisition

## Identity

- ID: `cgm_patient_acquisition`
- Version: `1.0`
- Active: yes
- Description: Book discovery meetings with suppliers billing CGM who want more qualified, eligible patients and faster intake.

## Ideal Customer Profile

- Industries: DME suppliers and diabetic supply providers billing CGM through Medicare, Medicare Advantage, or commercial payers.
- Company types: Independent CGM suppliers, diabetic supply companies, and pharmacies with a DME arm.
- Company size: 3–100 employees; owner, president, or business-development lead is the decision maker.
- Geography: United States; priority FL, TX, CA, GA.
- Exclusions: Not enrolled to bill CGM, OIG LEIE exclusion, lead resellers without fulfillment, or unwillingness to follow physician-order/documentation rules.

## Qualification Criteria

### Business fit — required

Evidence: current CGM billing status, brands, payers, and monthly volume. Pass when enrolled to bill CGM with at least one named payer type.

### Relevant need — required

Evidence: qualified-patient shortfall or lead-to-shipped-order fallout. Pass when a volume goal is missed or an acquisition/intake problem is named.

### Eligibility and process capability — required

Evidence: eligibility verification, physician orders, and documentation process. Pass when a working process or clear implementation plan exists.

### Authority and timing — required

Evidence: role and decision process. Pass when the speaker decides or commits to include the decision maker.

## Meeting Objectives

1. Confirm payers, brands, monthly new-patient volume, and fallout points.
2. Understand acquisition channels, cost per patient, and gaps.
3. Connect eligibility screening, physician outreach, and follow-up to the workflow.
4. Secure a pilot, proposal, or dated decision-maker follow-up.

## Opener Scorecard

- Booking quality: decision maker, date/time, and CGM billing status confirmed.
- Information accuracy: no unapproved claims about coverage or results.
- Expectation setting: Prime Ad, purpose, and length are clear.
- Pre-meeting capture: payers, volume, vendors, and decision maker recorded.

## Closer Scorecard

- Discovery: eligibility, order completion, cost per patient, and vendors.
- Presentation: connected to stated fallout and volume problems.
- Objection handling: compliance, quality, and pricing without overpromising.
- Qualification: billing status, need, process, and authority supported by evidence.
- Closing: pilot, proposal, or dated follow-up with named owner.

## Report Rules

- Support facts with transcript evidence and mark unknowns explicitly.
- Interest is not commitment.
- Record payer, volume, and cost figures exactly as stated.
- Do not infer coverage or eligibility.
